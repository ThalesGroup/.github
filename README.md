## 🛡️ Open Source: building tomorrow, together

Open Source is more than a software development method: it's a movement built on collaboration, transparency, and sharing. By fostering collective innovation, it lets everyone contribute, learn, and improve projects used worldwide.

At Thales, this strategic approach **accelerates innovation, strengthens security, and attracts top talent**. Open Source Software & Hardware are major concerns for the Group, and this GitHub organization is where we foster collaboration with the communities that make it possible.

Our mindset is simple: **Use → Contribute → Publish.**

## 🏆 Recognized for it

On **10 December 2025**, Thales received the **"Best Open Source Strategy"** award at the Open Source Experience trade show, presented by the CNLL (Conseil National du Logiciel Libre). It rewards five years of work by the Group's Technical Directorate: a structured transformation, mature internal processes, real impact in the Open Source community, and the commitment of teams across the company.

## 🌍 Why it matters to us

- **Security by transparency** — open code can be audited by the community, enabling faster detection and correction of vulnerabilities. Essential for a company that places trust and security at the heart of its business.
- **Sovereignty & independence** — reducing dependency on proprietary vendors, mastering our source code, and building on open standards.
- **Faster, better innovation** — collaborative development accelerates the maturity of the building blocks we rely on, from cybersecurity to cloud-native infrastructure to embedded hardware.
- **Talent attraction & retention** — engineers who contribute to Open Source stay engaged, keep their skills sharp, and take pride in work the whole world can see.
- **Inner Source as a bridge** — the same open, collaborative practices applied *inside* Thales first, breaking silos between entities before code is shared with the world.

## 🏗️ Open Source Hardware & RISC-V

Thales is a committed member of the **RISC-V** ecosystem, contributing to an open, royalty-free processor architecture that strengthens Europe's technological sovereignty in critical embedded systems — aerospace, space, automotive, rail, security, and defence. Our teams work on projects such as the CORE-V CVA6 processor developed by the OpenHW Group.

## ⚙️ How we govern it

Every Thales Open Source **publication** (sharing an internal asset) or **contribution** (fixing/improving an external project) goes through the same lightweight, structured process, reviewed by:

- a **manager**, to check alignment with business interests;
- a **technical expert**, to review code and architecture;
- the **Software Compliance team**, to manage IP, licensing and export-control risk;
- a **legal expert**, to validate the license choice.

Once approved, the project is published here — **the single entry point for all Thales Open Source assets**.
